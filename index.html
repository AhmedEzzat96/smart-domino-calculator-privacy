Privacy Policy

Smart Domino Calculator helps you read domino tiles and keep score. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Effective September 22, 2026

Information the app accesses

Camera. The app requests camera access so it can capture domino tiles and recognize their pips. Camera frames and photos are processed on your device for this purpose. The app does not upload them to a server or use them for advertising.

Information you create in the app. The current game, completed games, scores, preferences, and language selection are stored locally on your device so the app can resume games and show Past Games.

Information we collect

Smart Domino Calculator does not require an account and does not collect personal information on a server. We do not operate analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services in the app. We do not sell or share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Because the app does not maintain a remote account or cloud copy, there is no server-side profile to request or delete.

Third-party services

The app uses Apple platform services and Flutter software libraries to provide camera access, local storage, and the app experience. These libraries do not change the app's local-processing design. Apple may process information under Apple's own terms when you use Apple services or the App Store.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us through the support page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated with any revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit Smart Domino Calculator Support.
